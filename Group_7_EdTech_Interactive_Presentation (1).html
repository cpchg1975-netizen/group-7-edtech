🟣 Wordwall

Interactive & Flexible
Platform / Operating SystemWeb-based; works in modern browsers on computers, tablets and phones.
Supports collaborationStudents can participate individually, in teacher-led activities or through assignments.
Teacher FriendlyVery accessible: templates, editing tools and ready-made activities make preparation easier.
Monitor student resultsTeachers can use assignments to collect and review student results.
CostFree access is available; additional templates and features depend on the plan.
Target GroupSchool learners and language learners across different ages and levels.
Skills developedVocabulary, spelling, matching, recall, sentence practice and some speaking activities.
Registration RequiredTeacher: Yes. Students: usually can access activities/assignments through a link or code.
Limitations on Free PlansFree accounts have limits on templates/resources; exact limits can change.
Interesting FeatureOne activity can be transformed into another template, making the same content reusable.

⭐ Our Final Choice: Wordwall

“As an English teacher, I would choose Wordwall as the best option among Wordwall, Kahoot, Liveworksheets, Snorkl, and Wayground.”

Wordwall is extremely versatile because it allows teachers to create more than 30 different activity types, such as quizzes, matching games, word searches, flashcards, and speaking activities using the same content. It also offers printable versions of activities, which is very useful when internet access is limited. In addition, teachers can easily modify or transform one activity into another format with a single click, saving valuable preparation time. The platform contains millions of ready-made resources that can be adapted to different English levels and skills. For EFL classrooms, Wordwall provides an excellent balance between engagement, flexibility, assessment, and ease of use.
